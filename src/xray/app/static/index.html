02 · Role fingerprints

Each role has its own skill signature

Roles are assigned by transparent title rules. Small roles are flagged: with fewer than 30 postings, a share can move a lot when one posting changes.

03 · Cities

Where the demand sits

Share of postings in each city that mention the overall top skills. Darker means a higher share. Only cities with at least 50 postings get their own row.

04 · Trends

Rising and falling, measured honestly

Trends need real accumulated days and are never backfilled. Changes are measured on a balanced panel (only employers fetched OK every day). Significance comes from new postings only, using Fisher's exact test with Benjamini-Hochberg correction.

05 · Seniority classifier

Guess the level from the duties alone

Paste a job description. The title and every seniority keyword are removed before the model sees it, so it has to infer senior vs below-senior from the responsibilities and requirements. It is a fine-tuned DistilBERT, int8 ONNX on CPU.

    07 · Method

    From raw postings to this page

      No synthetic dataNothing is mocked or backfilled. Every posting keeps provenance to the raw API bytes (sha256).
      Rules you can readSkills, regions, roles and labels come from YAML rules in the repo. Rejected matches are kept and flagged.
      Gaps stay visibleFailed boards, missing days and low coverage are reported, never imputed.
      Honest modelsA fine-tuned transformer is compared with a linear baseline and a majority floor, with CIs and McNemar.
